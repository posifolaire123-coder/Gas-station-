Gas station first, but people stay for the tacos

Pleak Korner started as a fuel stop with an ice cooler out front, and somewhere along the way the taco window became the real reason people pull in. It's still the same trompo, the same hand-pressed tortillas, the same guy behind the counter who has your order figured out before you say it.

The inside won't win any awards — it smells like a working store because it is one — but that's never been the point. The point is you can fill your tank, grab a cold drink, cash a money order, and eat better than you expected to, all without leaving the lot.
